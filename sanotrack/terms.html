In short

  • Your check-ins, mood, symptoms, medications, measurements, appointments, and notes are stored in a private database on your phone.
  • There is no SanoTrack account and no cloud sync. We never receive your health data.
  • Your health data leaves the device only when you export or share it, and it goes wherever you choose.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics receive anonymous usage counts and crash reports, never your health data. You can turn them off in Settings → About.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat handle purchases. They see purchase details, never your health record.

1Who we are

SanoTrack is made by Vexorun Labs (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy explains how the SanoTrack Android app handles information. It applies to the app and to these legal pages.

It does not cover Google Play, RevenueCat, Firebase, or any other service you choose to use alongside SanoTrack. Those services have their own privacy policies. For support correspondence and the limited diagnostics and purchase information described below, Vexorun Labs is the data controller.

2What stays on your device

Everything you record is stored in SanoTrack's private storage on your phone, in a local database. It is not copied to a SanoTrack server, because there is no SanoTrack server for health data.

  • Check-ins and notesYour daily mood, the time of your check-in, and any free-text notes you add.
  • MoodThe mood rating you record with each check-in.
  • SymptomsSymptom names, severity, dates, and notes, including custom symptoms if you use Pro.
  • Measurements and custom trackersReadings such as blood pressure or weight, and numeric trackers you create, including any target range.
  • MedicationsNames, doses, schedules, refill counts if you add them, and whether each dose was taken or skipped.
  • AppointmentsClinicians, locations, dates, visit notes, and appointment history.
  • Medical IDConditions, allergies, and an emergency contact, if you choose to add them.
  • Settings and remindersDisplay preferences, reminder times, app-lock settings, onboarding state, and your diagnostics preference.

Many of these fields are free text. They can contain any sensitive detail you type, so treat your phone and any export like you would a paper health record.

3What we never send

SanoTrack does not send your health record to us or to Firebase. Specifically, we do not:

  • create an account or profile, or ask for your name, email address, or phone number;
  • sync your health data to a cloud service or keep a server-side copy;
  • include advertising SDKs, use the Android advertising ID, or track you across other apps;
  • access your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or body sensors;
  • send your check-ins, mood, symptoms, measurements, medications, appointments, Medical ID, notes, or other entries to analytics or crash-reporting services; or
  • sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Those tools do receive anonymous diagnostics, described in section 4. They receive event names and technical information, never the contents of your health record. We do not use them for profiling or advertising.

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. Please do not send health details you do not want us to have.

4Analytics and crash reports

SanoTrack uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google LLC, to understand which features are used and to find and fix crashes. Both are on by default. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → About → Share anonymous diagnostics.

Usage events

For the events SanoTrack logs, Firebase Analytics receives the event name only. SanoTrack does not send event parameters, a user ID, your name or email, or any value you enter in the app. The current events are:

  • app_open — the app started.
  • onboarding_completed — you finished onboarding.
  • first_check_in and check_in_completed — your first or a later check-in was saved. These do not include your mood, symptoms, notes, or any other entry.
  • quota_warn_shown and quota_exhausted — the free monthly limit is near or has been reached.
  • paywall_viewed, paywall_viewed_quota, paywall_viewed_trends, paywall_viewed_custom, paywall_viewed_export, and paywall_viewed_manual — the Pro paywall was shown, with the reason it opened.
  • paywall_plan_selected — you selected a plan on the paywall.
  • purchase_started, purchase_success, purchase_restored, and revenuecat_ready — a purchase, restore, or purchase-service start completed.
  • export_used — you exported data. This does not include the exported content.
  • share_app_tapped and share_summary_tapped — you opened a share sheet.
  • review_requested — the in-app review prompt was requested.

Firebase also collects limited technical information automatically: a random app-instance ID, app version and lifecycle or session data, device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, language, and a coarse location derived from your IP address. Google Play Billing may report standard purchase events such as product ID, product name, and price. We have disabled collection of the Android advertising ID and Android ID in the app.

Crash reports

If SanoTrack crashes or an error occurs, Crashlytics may receive a stack trace, a technical error message and code, app version, device model, operating system, available memory and disk space, whether the device is rooted, the Firebase installation ID, and similar technical details. We do not attach your health record, set a user ID, or add custom logs or keys to these reports.

How we use it

We use diagnostics only to measure aggregate feature use, diagnose failures, and improve reliability. Firebase transmits this data over encrypted connections. We do not use it for advertising or automated decisions, and we do not sell it.

Your control and retention

Turning off Share anonymous diagnostics stops Analytics and Crashlytics collection, clears queued analytics events, and deletes unsent crash reports. Reports already sent are not recalled: Google retains Analytics event data for up to 14 months and Crashlytics crash data for 90 days. Clearing all data resets this preference to its default, which is on, so turn it off again after onboarding if you want to opt out.

Because diagnostics are tied to a random installation ID rather than your name or account, we may not be able to identify your data for an access or deletion request. You can always stop future collection with the setting above. See Firebase's privacy and security information and the Google Privacy Policy.

5When data leaves the device

Your health data moves off your device only when you ask it to. Anonymous diagnostics are separate, automatic, and described in section 4; they never include your health record.

You can move health data off the device in four ways:

Backups and spreadsheets

JSON backup and import are free. CSV export is part of Pro. SanoTrack opens Android's system file picker so you choose the destination. There is no OAuth and no Drive API connection. We do not receive the file or gain access to your Drive.

Doctor report and Medical ID

The PDF doctor report and the Medical ID PDF are generated on your device. You decide whether to save or share them.

Summary card

The weekly summary card is a PNG image generated on your device and shared through Android's share sheet.

Calendar files

Appointments can be exported as an .ics calendar file. Like the others, it goes wherever you send it.

Once a file leaves SanoTrack, it is outside our control. The app, storage provider, or person you send it to handles it under their own terms. Exports are plaintext, not password-protected, so treat them as sensitive documents.

Uninstalling SanoTrack removes its private database. Files you exported or shared before uninstalling remain wherever you saved or sent them, and diagnostics already sent to Firebase are not deleted by uninstalling.

6Purchases and subscriptions

Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment, and we never receive your card or bank details.

We use RevenueCat to check and restore Pro entitlements. RevenueCat receives an app-generated customer ID, purchase receipts and transaction identifiers, subscription status, and limited technical information such as platform, app version, and IP address. It does not receive your health data.

Google and RevenueCat process purchase information under their own policies, which may involve transfers to the United States and other countries. You can read them here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Terms of Service
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Restoring purchases uses the Google account you bought Pro with. We do not create a separate SanoTrack account.

7Permissions

SanoTrack asks only for permissions it needs. You can decline most of them and still use the app.

  • NotificationsLocal check-in and medication reminders. No push notification server is involved.
  • Run after restartLets Android reschedule reminders after your phone reboots.
  • InternetUsed for Google Play billing, RevenueCat entitlement checks, and Firebase diagnostics. It is not used to upload health data.
  • Files and foldersWhen you export or import, Android's system picker gives the app access only to the file or folder you choose. SanoTrack does not request broad storage access.
  • BiometricsIf you turn on the optional app lock, Android handles the fingerprint or face check. SanoTrack never receives your biometric data.

You can change notification and app-lock permissions at any time in Android Settings or in SanoTrack.

8Keeping data safe

SanoTrack is built to reduce exposure:

  • The database is encrypted at rest with SQLCipher, using a random 256-bit key generated on your device and kept in Android Keystore. The key is not included in exports.
  • The database lives in the app's private sandbox, which other apps cannot normally read.
  • Android's file-based encryption protects device storage on modern phones.
  • Android cloud backup is turned off for SanoTrack, so the health database is not copied to Google Drive.
  • There is no account or cloud copy of your health record.
  • The optional app lock requires biometric authentication to open the app.

Exports are not encrypted by SanoTrack. JSON, CSV, PDF, PNG, and .ics files are plaintext and can contain sensitive health information, so treat them like a paper health record. The database key is protected by Android Keystore, but on a rooted or compromised device, or a device left unlocked, app data may still be accessible while the app is running. Keep Android updated, use a screen lock, and be careful where you save exports.

No storage system is completely secure. If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please email vexorunlabs@gmail.com and give us a reasonable chance to fix it before disclosing it publicly.

9Retention and deletion

Your health data stays on your device until you delete it. Diagnostics and purchase records are handled separately.

  • Clear all data. In SanoTrack, go to Settings → Clear all data. This erases the health database and app settings on that device, including the diagnostics preference, and returns the app to onboarding. It cannot be undone.
  • Uninstall. Removing the app deletes its private app data, subject to how Android handles uninstalls. Diagnostics already sent to Firebase are not deleted by uninstalling.
  • Exported files. SanoTrack cannot delete files you saved or shared elsewhere. Delete them in the app or service where you put them.
  • Diagnostics. Firebase Analytics event data is retained for up to 14 months and Crashlytics crash data for 90 days. Turn off Share anonymous diagnostics to stop new collection and delete unsent crash reports. Email us to request deletion of data already sent; because it is tied to a random installation ID, we may not be able to identify it.
  • No SanoTrack server copy. There is no SanoTrack server copy of your health record, so there is nothing there to delete. Purchase records held by Google and RevenueCat, and diagnostics held by Google, are governed by their retention policies; contact us if you need help with a request.

Clearing app data does not cancel a Pro subscription, and it resets Share anonymous diagnostics to on. Cancel Pro in Google Play and turn diagnostics off again if you want to opt out.

10Children's privacy

SanoTrack is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. We do not knowingly collect health information or account details from children. The diagnostics described in section 4 are limited to technical and usage information and are not used for advertising. Because health data is stored locally, a parent or guardian can delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. If you believe a child's information has been handled improperly, contact us.

11Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal information, and to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold your health data on a server, you can exercise most of these rights directly:

  • Access and portability. View your data in the app, or export it. JSON backup and import are free; CSV export is a Pro feature.
  • Correction. Edit or delete individual entries in the app.
  • Deletion. Use Settings → Clear all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Diagnostics. Turn off Share anonymous diagnostics at any time. You can also email us to ask about access to or deletion of diagnostics; because the data is tied to a random installation ID rather than your name, we may not be able to identify it.
  • Purchase data. Contact Google Play or RevenueCat. Email us if you need help finding the right route.

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right. California residents have the same choices: there is no sale or advertising use to opt out of.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
