Privacy Policy — VexoScan: Scanner, OCR & PDF

Developer: Vexorun Labs · Contact: vexorunlabs@google.com
App: VexoScan: Scanner, OCR & PDF (Android, package com.vexorunlabs.vexoscan)
Effective date: September 14, 2026 · Last updated: September 14, 2026

In short: VexoScan processes your documents entirely on your device. We run no accounts, no servers for your files, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK in this version. Your scans, photos, page images, and OCR text stay in the app’s private storage until you share, export, or delete them. Third-party SDKs used for ads (Google AdMob) and purchases (RevenueCat with Google Play Billing) may collect limited device and transaction data under their own policies, described below.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide (stored only on your device)

  • Scanned documents, imported images/PDFs, page images, OCR text, and document titles — created when you scan, import, convert, or edit. Stored in the app’s private on-device database and storage.
  • App settings (e.g. PDF preferences, favorites) — stored on-device via local preferences.

We do not ask for your name, email, address, or any account details. There is no sign-in.

1.2 Information we do not collect

  • No account or profile data.
  • No analytics or product-usage tracking SDK.
  • No crash-reporting SDK in this version.
  • No location data.
  • No contact lists, call logs, or SMS data.
  • We never upload your documents, page images, or OCR text to our servers — we operate none for this app.

2. Device permissions and how they are used

Permission / access Purpose Notes
Camera (via the built-in ML Kit document scanner) Capture document scans only when you tap Scan. No background camera use. You can also import files without using the camera.
Photos / media / files (system image picker and file picker) Import images or PDFs you explicitly choose; save or export files you request. Access is user-initiated only; the app cannot browse your gallery on its own.
Internet Serve ads (free tier), validate Pro purchases, load on-device ML models if required by the OS. Your documents are not transmitted by us.
Google Play Billing Process one-time Pro unlock purchases. Handled by Google Play and RevenueCat; we never see your full payment details.
Advertising ID (via Google Mobile Ads, free tier) Deliver, measure, and help personalize ads as described in Section 4. Removed from ad use if you opt out of ad personalization or buy Pro (ads are disabled for Pro).

3. On-device processing (scanning & OCR)

Document capture uses Google ML Kit Document Scanner and text recognition uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition, both running on-device. Your document images and recognized text are processed locally and are not sent to Vexorun Labs. Google’s handling of on-device ML processing is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and the ML Kit Terms. Because models run locally, scanning and OCR work without us ever seeing your content.

4. Advertising (free tier)

The free version of VexoScan shows banner and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. Buying the Pro lifetime unlock disables ads.

  • AdMob may collect or receive: device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), approximate location (derived from IP, where permitted), app interactions (e.g. ad impressions, taps), and diagnostic data to serve, measure, and prevent abuse of ads.
  • Ads may be personalized based on your Advertising ID and Google ad settings.
  • You can control personalization: on Android, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization, and use Google’s ad settings. See also How Google uses data when you use our partners’ apps and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Our AdMob usage in test builds uses Google’s sample ad units; release builds use our production ad units. Either way, ad data handling is by Google under its policies.

5. Purchases (Pro unlock)

Pro is a one-time lifetime unlock sold through Google Play Billing and managed via RevenueCat (entitlement pro).

  • RevenueCat may process: purchase records (product ID, purchase token, entitlement status), an anonymous app user ID it generates, and limited device and app data (e.g. OS version, app version, device identifiers) needed to validate and restore your entitlement across devices.
  • Google Play processes the payment itself; we never receive your card number or full billing details.
  • See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy / Google Payments Privacy Notice.
  • Restore: reinstalling or switching devices re-validates your entitlement through RevenueCat/Google Play; no Vexorun Labs account is created.

6. Sharing and exports (only when you ask)

When you tap Share, Save, Export, or Open & view, the file is handed to Android (system share sheet, file saver, or viewer) or written to a location you choose. From that point, the receiving app or location handles the file under its own policy. We do not transmit your files anywhere else.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Your documents: kept on your device until you delete them in the app (deleting a document permanently removes its pages and derived files) or uninstall the app (which removes the app’s private storage).
  • Free-tier page counter: derived locally from your on-device library; deleting documents lowers it.
  • No server copies: because we store no user files on servers, there is nothing to request deletion of from us. Ad and purchase data held by Google/RevenueCat is retained under their policies; exercise those rights via their channels or your Google account.

8. Security

Your files live in the app’s private sandboxed storage and local database, protected by Android’s app sandbox. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, so protect your device with a screen lock and be cautious when sharing or exporting sensitive documents. Backup apps or OS-level backups you enable may copy app data outside our control.

9. Your rights

  • Access / correction / deletion of your documents and settings: available directly in the app at any time (view, rename, delete); uninstalling removes all local data.
  • EEA / UK (GDPR): our on-device processing needs no personal-data transfer to us. For ad and purchase data processed by Google and RevenueCat as independent controllers/processors, you may exercise access, objection, restriction, portability, and erasure rights through them and your Google account settings.
  • California / US state laws: we do not sell or share your personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not use sensitive personal information for inferring characteristics. Ad personalization choices are available via your device’s Ads settings (Section 4).
  • Contact us at vexorunlabs@google.com for privacy questions; we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children

VexoScan is a general-purpose productivity app not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party SDK flow (e.g. an ad tap), contact us and we will help direct the request to the relevant provider. Parents and guardians can use Google Play parental controls and device supervision tools.

11. International transfers

We transfer no user files anywhere. Google and RevenueCat may process ad and purchase data on servers outside your country (including the United States) under their own safeguards and policies linked above.
